# Malcolm Lismore Photography Website

This is a web-based portfolio and enquiry platform for **Malcolm Lismore**, a freelance photographer based on the North West coast of Scotland. The website showcases his stunning landscape, wildlife, and event photography, providing a professional platform for potential clients to inquire about his services for weddings and special events.

## Features

- **Home Page**: Introduction to the website and easy navigation to other sections.
- **About Page**: Information about Malcolm, his experience, and his passion for photography.
- **Pricing Page**: Details of Malcolm's photography pricing for different services.
- **Photography Gallery**: A showcase of Malcolm’s landscape, wildlife, and event photographs. The gallery is designed to be easily expandable for future uploads.
- **Enquiry Form**: A form for users to submit booking inquiries, including their contact information, location, and event details.

## Technologies Used

- **HTML**: Structure and content of the website.
- **CSS**: Styling for responsive and engaging design.
- **JavaScript**: For dynamic behavior and form validation.
- **PHP**: Back-end support for handling enquiries and other dynamic content (if applicable).
